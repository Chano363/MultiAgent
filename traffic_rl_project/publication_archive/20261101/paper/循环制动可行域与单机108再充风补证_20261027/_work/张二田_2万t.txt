 Analysis and Application Research on Longitudinal Coupler Force of 20，000 t
         Heavy-haul Combined Train on Shuozhou-Huanghua Railway
                                                 Zhang Ertian
                       （CN Energy Shuohuang Railway Development Co.，Ltd. Suning，Hebei，062350）
Abstract：With the dynamic test data of 20，000 t heavy-haul combined train on Shuozhou-Huanghua Rail⁃
way，this paper researches the distribution rule and safety performance of dynamic indexes such as longitudi⁃
nal coupler force and longitudinal acceleration of 20，000 t heavy- haul combined train in different sections
of Shuozhou-Huanghua Railway，analyzes the influencing factors，and puts forward technical countermeasures
to reduce the safety risk of train operation.
Key Words：heavy-haul combined train；longitudinal coupler force；analysis；countermeasures
