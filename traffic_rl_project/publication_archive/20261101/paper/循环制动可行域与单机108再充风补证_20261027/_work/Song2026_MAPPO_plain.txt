The core challenge lies in achieving speed synchronization, distance maintenance, and overall energy efficiency optimization of multiple trains at the macroscopic operation level under strict safety constraints. Addressing the high-dimensionality, nonlinearity, and distributed decision-making characteristics of this problem, this paper proposes a fully data-driven multi-agent reinforcement learning framework. Abandoning reliance on precise analytical models, each train is modeled as an agent that autonomously learns cooperative control strategies through continuous trial-and-error interaction with the environment. This paper first establishes a detailed longitudinal dynamics model of heavy-haul trains as a high-fidelity simulation environment for agent interaction. Furthermore, a training framework is designed centered on the Multi-Agent Proximal Policy Optimization algorithm, supplemented by potential-field-based safety guidance rewards and multi-objective optimization rewards.
