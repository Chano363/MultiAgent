           纵向冲动的影响 *

       摘    要：为研究 3 万吨重载列车合理的编组方式，文章基于纵向动力学与气体流动理论，建立 3 万吨
  重载列车纵向动力学模型，利用该模型分析不同编组方式列车空气制动系统传递特性；在此基础上，选取
  3 种典型运行线路，仿真分析不同编组方式列车循环制动工况下的纵向冲动。结果表明：列车长度会影响
  列车空气制动系统同步性能，尾部添加机车可以有效提升制动同步性，相较于传统尾部列尾装置，缓解延
  迟时间减少约 58.8%；纵向冲动由列车制动不同步性及坡道差共同作用产生，列车循环制动通过 V 型坡时，
  坡度差不应超过 10.5‰；“八轴机车 +108 辆 C80+ 八轴机车 +108 辆 C80+ 八轴机车 +108 辆 C80+ 八轴机车”
  的编组方式在各线路条件下纵向冲动均保持较低水平，可为后续 3 万吨重载列车试验及常态化运营的编组
  选择提供参考。

            Inﬂuence of formation method on longitudinal impulse of
           30 000-ton heavy-haul train during cyclic braking conditions
      FAN Yunxin1, 2, CHEN Zhe1, 2, 3, LIN Runze3, ZENG Fanjun2, ZOU Yimin2, LING Liang3
   ( 1. State Key Laboratory of Heavy-duty and Express High-power Electric Locomotive, Zhuzhou 412001, China;
                            2. CRRC Zhuzhou Locomotive Co., Ltd., Zhuzhou 412001, China;
  3. State Key Laboratory of Rail Transit Vehicle System, Southwest Jiaotong University, Chengdu 610031, China )

       Abstract: To study the reasonable formation method of a 30 000-ton heavy-haul train, a longitudinal
  dynamic model of the train is established based on longitudinal dynamics and gas ﬂow theory in this paper. Using
  this model, the transmission characteristics of the air brake system in trains with different formation methods are
  analyzed. On this basis, three typical operational routes are selected, and the longitudinal impulses of trains under
  different formation methods during cyclic braking conditions are simulated and analyzed. The results indicate that
  the length of the train affects the synchronization performance of the air brake system. Adding locomotives at the
  rear can effectively improve braking synchronization, reducing the delay time by approximately 58.8% compared to
  traditional rear-end train conﬁgurations. Longitudinal impulses are generated by the asynchrony of train braking and
  grade differences. When the train is braking on a V-shaped slope, the grade difference should not exceed 10.5‰.
