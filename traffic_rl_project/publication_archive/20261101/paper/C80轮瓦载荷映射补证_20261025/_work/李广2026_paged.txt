An Investigation on the Tread Braking Heat Distribution
Law Between Railway Freight Car Wheels and Brake Shoes
， ， ， ，
LI Guang WU Yi PEI Dingfeng JI Yuan LI Zixuan
（ ， ，
Metals and Chemistry Research Institute China Academy of Railway Sciences Corporation Limited
， ）
Beijing 100081 China
Abstract：
To clarify the heat distribution law between railway freight car wheels and brake shoes during
-
tread braking, two typical domestic high friction composite brake shoes were taken as the research
objects. The measured temperature curves of the brake shoes during tread braking were obtained through
bench tests, and the heat distribution between the wheels and brake shoes was investigated via finite
-
element simulation of the brake shoe temperature field. The results show that for high friction composite
brake shoes, the proportion of heat absorbed by the brake shoes ranges from 2% to 6%, while the wheels
- -
absorb 94%~98% of the heat. Compared with LH1 type brake shoes, the LH2 type brake shoes exhibit a
slightly higher heat absorption ratio. This is attributed to the matched wheels' thermal capacity and the
-
friction coefficient of the brake shoes: the 840 mm diameter wheels possess a lower thermal capacity than
- -
the 915 mm diameter wheels, and the friction coefficient of the LH2 type brake shoes is slightly higher,
-
both contributing to the higher heat absorption ratio of the LH2 type brake shoes. Furthermore, it is found
that as the braking heat input increases, the proportion of heat absorbed by the brake shoes tends to
decrease, with more braking heat being borne by the wheels. These findings provide a theoretical basis for
the thermal load assessment of railway freight car wheels and the optimization of brake shoe materials.
Keywords：
railway freight car; wheel; brake shoe; tread braking heat; finite element simulation; bench
test
