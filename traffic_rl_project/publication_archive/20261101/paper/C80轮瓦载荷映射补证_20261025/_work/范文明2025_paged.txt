重载列车长大下坡典型制动场景下的轮瓦温升特性
范文明 1，孟宪国 1，丁 颖 1，杨雨浩 2，吴 磊 2
（1. 国能铁路装备有限责任公司 北京 100011；2. 西南交通大学 轨道交通运载系统全国重点实验
室，四川 成都 610031）
摘 要：为研究重载列车长大下坡制动过程车轮和闸瓦温度场演变规律，分别建立重载铁路车轮、
闸瓦的有限元模型，并在常用制动、紧急制动、循环制动三种重载铁路典型制动场景下，采用所
建立的有限元模型分析了制动初速度、线路坡度、环境温度三大关键因素对重载列车车轮、闸瓦
制动温度场的影响。分析结果表明：制动初速度的提升会显著加剧车轮/闸瓦温升，在紧急制动场
景下极为明显，当初速度由80 km·h⁻¹增至120 km·h⁻¹时，轮瓦峰值温度分别从约223℃、225℃升
至 471℃、469℃, 升幅达 111%与 108%；在所仿真参数范围内，线路坡度与三类典型制动场景下
车轮、闸瓦的峰值温度均呈现近似线性增长趋势；环境温度变化对常用、紧急制动影响显著，在
50℃较-40℃环境下，常用制动轮瓦峰值温度增幅约36%与28%，但对长时间循环制动下车轮与闸
瓦的峰值温度几乎无影响，极端温差下最终峰值差异不足 2%。此外，不同制动场景下闸瓦温度
场分布特性存在明显差异：常用与紧急制动工况下高温区域集中于整个闸瓦摩擦接触面，而在循
环制动下闸瓦摩擦面则呈现“椭圆形”的局部高温区域。基于常用制动、紧急制动和循环制动三
类典型场景下的温度场演变规律分析表明，短时制动下过高初速度与陡坡循环制动可能是导致轮
瓦热损伤的关键风险工况。研究结果构建的分析模型为重载列车优化坡道限速策略、制动缓解时
机等操纵决策提供直接数据支撑，从而有效预防热致故障并提升运行安全性。
关键词：重载列车；温度场；有限元分析；踏面制动；长大坡道

Temperature characteristics of wheels and brake shoes during typical
braking on heavy-haul train long steep downgrades
FAN Wen-ming1, MENG Xian-guo1, DING Ying1, YANG Yu-hao2, WU Lei2
(1. China Energy Railway Equipment Co., Ltd, Beijing, 100011, China; 2. State Key Laboratory of Rail
Transit Vehicle System, Southwest Jiaotong University, Chengdu, 610031, Sichuan, China )
Abstract: To investigate the evolution patterns of wheel and brake shoe temperature fields during heavy-
haul train tread braking on long downhill sections, finite element models of heavy-haul railway wheels and
brake shoes were established, analyzing the impacts of initial braking speed, track gradient, and ambient
temperature under three typical braking scenarios—normal, emergency, and cyclic braking. Results
demonstrate that increased initial braking speed significantly intensifies wheel-brake shoe temperature rise,
which is particularly pronounced under emergency braking conditions; when the initial speed rises from
80 km·h⁻¹ to 120 km·h⁻¹, the peak temperatures of the wheel and brake shoe increase from approximately
223℃ and 225℃ to 471℃ and 469℃, representing rises of 111% and 108%, respectively. Within the
simulated parameter range, the track gradient shows an approximately linear growth trend in relation to the
peak temperatures of both the wheel and the brake shoe across the three typical braking scenarios.

Variations in ambient temperature exert a notable influence on normal and emergency braking; under
normal braking, for instance, the peak temperatures of the wheel and brake shoe increase by approximately
36% and 28% in a 50℃ environment compared to a -40℃environment. However, ambient temperature has
minimal impact on the peak temperatures of the wheel and brake shoe during prolonged cyclic braking,
with the final peak difference under extreme temperature conditions being less than 2%. Notably,
temperature distributions differ markedly: normal and emergency braking concentrate high-temperature
zones across the entire brake shoe friction surface, while cyclic braking forms elliptical high-temperature
regions. This study quantitatively reveals differential impact mechanisms of braking parameters on thermal
behavior, identifying excessive initial speed during short braking and steep-gradient cyclic braking as
critical conditions triggering thermal damage thresholds. The multiparameter coupling model delivers
direct data support for optimizing operational decisions (downhill speed limits and braking release timing),
thereby effectively preventing thermally induced failures and enhancing safety.

2.1.2 线路坡度的影响
线路坡度作为影响踏面制动热负荷的关键因

坡度条件。在初速度 100km·h-1 的常用制动工况
下，计算了车轮与闸瓦的温度场。据此绘制了轮瓦
峰值温度随时间演变的历程曲线，如图 11 所示。
图 11 结果表明，线路坡度越大，轮瓦温升速率越
快，峰值温度越高。其作用机制在于：坡度增大导
致车辆重力势能增加，根据能量守恒定律（式
(4)），需转化为摩擦热的能量增加，输入轮瓦的热
流密度随之增大，导致摩擦温度升高。在-24‰坡
度下，车轮踏面峰值温度达 290.68℃，闸瓦峰值温
度为 276.69℃，而在-10‰坡度下，车轮与闸瓦峰
值温度分别为 220.28℃，210.01℃。该工况下轮瓦
峰值温度分别上升70.4℃、66.68℃。
