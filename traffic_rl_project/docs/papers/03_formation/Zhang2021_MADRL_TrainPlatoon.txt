Multi-Agent Deep Reinforcement Learning Based
Consistency Control in Train Platoon Systems
Xinran Zhang, Gaofeng Nie, Wanli Ni, Hui Tian
State Key Laboratory of Networking and Switching Technology
Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications, Beijing 100876, China
Email: {ZhangXinran, niegaofeng, charleswall, tianhui }@bupt.edu.cn
Abstract—The train platoon (TP) in the urban rail transit
(URT) system has been regarded as a promising solution to
improve the transportation capacity between metro stations.
Moreover, TP has a great potential to effectively adapt to
the high dynamic ﬂow of passengers during major events or
festivals. Unfortunately, the promotion of TP is challenged due
to the auto-block system in the traditional URT system as well
as the complicated and dynamic operation environment. This
paper investigates a cooperative control method to realize the
consistency of TP in the premise of anti-interference. The multi-
agent system of TP based on leader-follower typology is designed
to achieve the operation goals in terms of distance and velocity.
A novel distributed multi-agent deep reinforcement learning
algorithm is proposed to obtain the optimal control strategy,
where the artiﬁcial potential ﬁeld is structured to provide prior
knowledge of the environment. Compared with the conventional
Q-learning based methods, simulation results validate that the
proposed algorithm not only beneﬁts the achievement of the
operation goal, but also prevents the unfavorable interference
from the external system.
Index Terms —Multi-agent deep reinforcement learning, train
platoons, artiﬁcial potential ﬁeld, cooperative control.
I. I NTRODUCTION
Communication-based train control system (CBTC) serves
as the core of urban rail transit (URT) system nowadays. Under
CBTC mechanism, two transportation problems urge to be
solved. Firstly, with the increasing pressure of overwhelming
passenger ﬂow, the capacity of railway transportation can
not satisfy the quantity of passengers virtually. Overload of
trains happens occasionally, which may threaten life security
of passengers. Secondly, regarded as the phenomenon of tidal
passenger ﬂows, the direction into the city requires more
trains than the counterpart in the morning peak. Instead, both
directions are allocated with identical numbers of train sets,
in which case demand exceeds supply in the direction of city-
entering and vice versa, causing transportation resource waste.
Based on aforementioned problems, a novel train platoon
(TP) system is structured to increase the transportation capac-
ity and accommodate the high dynamic ﬂow of passengers. In
order to shorten the distance between adjacent trains in TP ,
train-to-train is introduced as another communication method
in addition to train-to-infrastructure to reduce the communica-
tion delay [1]. However, the auto-block system of the control

center in CBTC restrains the relative distance of trains [2].
Therefore, auto-control based on commands from the control
center is possible to be substituted for smart control based on
artiﬁcial intelligence. To facilitate the matching between trains
and passengers, trains in TP utilize wireless communication
as a virtual coupler replacing the mechanical coupler so that
TP can enroll or deprive trains according to the quantity
of passengers ﬂexibly [3]. Nevertheless, the dynamic motion
of trains and the complicated environment pose a threat to
operation security of TP . In this paper, we consider operation
security is achieved if TP arrives at the destination successfully
without out-of-range accidents and break-down caused by
unpredictable interference.
Considering that TP serves as a multi-agent system (MAS),
deep reinforcement learning (DRL) is regarded as an appropri-
ate AI control method of TP in recent studies. Most existing
works adopt centralized DRL based on global information on
considerable states [4], whose dimension increases intensely
when participants enroll in TP , leading to unforeseen com-
putation complexity. Meanwhile, DRL lacks the prior knowl-
edge resulting in poor training efﬁciency. Motivated by the
aforementioned challenges of DRL, we propose a distributed
multi-agent deep reinforcement learning (multi-agent DRL) to
control agents locally and increase the training speed by the
pre-designed reward function. Compared with general DRL,
the proposed multi-agent DRL has greater advantage of saving
computation resource and improving training efﬁciency, which
is more suitable to the TP system with multiple agents.

operate vehicle platoons. In early studies, researchers focused
on the motion of platoons, including formulation of platoon
and path planning [5]. Recently, robust performance of vehicle

method. Considering possible interior error or exterior inter-
ference, control methods were developed by [6]–[8] to real-
ize error detection and correction. Speciﬁcally, Wang eta l .
proposed a distributed model predictive control algorithm to
tackle time-varying constraints derived from abnormal com-
munication by switching communication topology and control
topology [6]. Alotibi eta l . considered the critical threats of
false information from the platoon leader, and proposed a real-
time anomaly detection mechanism that combined statistical

B. Contributions
Due to the dynamic motion of multiple trains in the
complicated environment, achieving an efﬁcient cooperative
control of TP is a challenging problem. In this paper, we
propose a distributed reinforcement learning method to solve
the consistency control problem with security guarantee. The
main contributions of this paper are listed as follows:
• We formulate a multi-objective problem in the TP system
based on a leader-follower typology, where an artiﬁcial
potential ﬁeld (APF) method is adopted to unify a variety
of force. Unlike APF in previous studies, we design
twin potential ﬁelds in terms of distance and velocity
difference between adjacent trains separately, and the
value of potential ﬁeld force is limited to suit for reality.
• By designing reward function based on APF to provide
prior knowledge and improve training efﬁciency, we
develop a distributed multi-agent DQN algorithm which
allows agents to prevent overwhelming state information.
Different from the past studies, the reward function is
built according to characteristics of different states, which
accelerates the training process to a large degree.

II. S
YSTEM MODEL AND PROBLEM FORMULA TION
In this paper, we focus on the entire journey of TP from
one destination to another. In the premise of the successful
arrival at destination, the operation goal of TP is to achieve
the consistence of velocity and distance between adjacent
trains simultaneously, which is deﬁned as the cooperative
consistency control of TP . The operation goal of TP is able
to be converted into the operation goal of followers, which
is to achieve zero velocity difference and the predeﬁned
goal distance simultaneously between every follower and the
predecessor. Note that the predeﬁned goal distance is identical
in terms of every follower.

A. System Model
As illustrated in Fig. 1(a), the leader-follower typology is
applied to the formation of TP . N denotes the size of TP
indexed by N = {1, 2, ..., N}. Members of TP are divided into
leaders and followers. Leaders play a role of leading direction,
including trains in TP except the N -th train. Followers are
the trains behind leaders, including trains in TP except the
ﬁrst train. To be simpliﬁed, any follower mentioned below is
denoted as the n-th train, and ∀n ∈N \ {1} if not instructed
particularly. With the exception of the ﬁrst and the N -th
train, every train in TP is regarded as not only a leader but
also a follower, which follows the predecessor and leads the
successor. Every follower communicates with the predecessor
leader solely. Followers depend on the operation states of their
leaders to decide the control strategies.
We model the complex dynamics environment of TP as an
artiﬁcial potential ﬁeld (APF) where potential ﬁeld force is
the only variety of force that trains take [16]. Two aspects
are considered to design the APF. Firstly, the zero potential
point is required to be the operation goal of TP . Considering
the local optimization of APF, the potential ﬁeld function
requires a sole minimum. Secondly, the potential ﬁeld force
function is required to reach zero at the zero potential point. In
other words, trains maintain the goal operation state. In other
situations, the potential ﬁeld force of a train points to the zero
potential point, which means the potential ﬁeld force can be
attractive or repulsive. The potential ﬁeld force drives TP to
achieve the operation goal. Considering practical situation, the
potential ﬁeld force function is required to be bounded. Based
on the aforementioned aspects, ln(cosh(·)) is selected as the
potential ﬁeld function and ∇ ln(cosh(·))= tanh(·) serves as
the potential ﬁeld force function [1]. In view of the operation
goal, twin potential ﬁelds are designed and constitute the entire
artiﬁcial environment.

the distance between adjacent trains is xn− 1,n=xn− 1 − xn.

and the distance error between them is en− 1,n = xn− 1,n −
dgoal. vn is deﬁned as the velocity of the n-th train, and the ve-
locity difference between adjacent trains is vn− 1,n=vn− 1 − vn.

III. M
ULTI -AGENT DEEP Q-N ETWORK FOR COOPERA TIVE
CONSISTENCY CONTROL
To address the above problems, we turn to developing
DRL-based control method for followers, which is capable
to search the global optimal strategy. However, due to zero
prior knowledge of environment and random action-choosing
at the start of training, DRL is of low training efﬁciency.
Moreover, to deal with the MAS, the general DRL produces
overwhelming data leading to excessively complex computa-
tion. To solve these problems, we develop a distributed multi-
agent DRL algorithm. Distributed DRL means every agent in
TP is trained separately and has personal states, actions and
rewards differently, instead of being trained jointly and sharing
identical states and rewards. Compared with general DRL, the
reward function of multi-agent DRL is delicately designed in
accordance with APF, by means of which APF plays a role of
prior knowledge provider. Deep Q-Network (DQN) is adopted
due to the signiﬁcant competence in ﬁtting nonlinear function
and saving storage space.

B. Multi-Agent Deep Q-Network
As illustrated in Fig.4, every agent possesses a personal

C. Extension to Bi-direction Communication
As illustrated in Fig.1(b) , in order to prevent trains
from out-of-range communication during operation, we further
develop a cooperative consistency control method with bi-
direction communication between adjacent trains. Every train
except the N -th train in TP communicates with the successor
in addition to the predecessor so as to obtains operation states
information. In other words, every train except the N -th train
is attached with perception ability to the successor. When the
distance between adjacent trains is nearly out-of-range, both
of them can adjust acceleration automatically to shorten the
distance rather than the successor solely, which strengthens
the operation security for the most part.
We allow the ﬁrst train to adjust the operation state automat-
ically by substituting A TO for RL. In order to guarantee the
prescriptive schedule and route of TP , we introduce a virtual
leader as a predecessor of the ﬁrst train, which serves as a
follower. The virtual leader is assumed to be controlled by
A TO, the operation state of which is identical to the ﬁrst train
with uni-direction communication.

V. C
ONCLUSION
In this paper, we investigated the cooperative consistency
control problem in TP system. Considering local optimization
of traditional control method and a lack of operation security, a
novel multi-agent DQN algorithm based on APF was proposed
to solve zero prior knowledge problem and obtain the global
optimal control strategy. Furthermore, the bi-direction commu-
nication method of trains was proposed to prevent TP from the
out-of-range accident speciﬁcally. Simulation results showed
that the proposed multi-agent DQN algorithm could not only
contribute to the achievement of the operation goal, but also
improve the anti-interference capability of the control method.
Meanwhile, out-of-range is reduced based on the proposed
algorithm. Moreover, the proposed bi-direction communication
method could take precaution against the out-of-range accident
for the most part.
