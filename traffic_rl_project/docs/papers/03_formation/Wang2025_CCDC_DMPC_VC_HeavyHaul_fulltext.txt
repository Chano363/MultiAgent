Abstract—In
this paper, a virtual coupling control strategy
for heavy-haul trains (HHTs) based on a multi-mass-point
model is proposed, aiming to further enhance train capacity
and operational efficiency. Compared to traditional single-mass-
point model, the proposed multi-mass-point model provides a
moreaccuraterepresentationofHHTdynamics.Aoptimization
problem is formulated to minimize speed tracking error and

the lead HHT and each following HHT within the framework
of distributed model predictive control (DMPC). The lead train
tracksanoptimalreferencetrajectory,whileeachfollowingtrain
tracks the speed of its preceding train and maintains a safe yet
smallest possible tracking interval. Finally, the effectiveness of
the method is verified by numerical simulation, demonstrating
its excellent performance in speed tracking, minimizing in-train
force and interval maintenance, even in the presence of random
disturbances.

V. CONCLUSION
In order to enhance the capacity and operational efficiency
of HHTs, we have proposed a virtual coupling strategy for
HHTsbasedonamulti-mass-pointmodelinthispaper.DMPC
controllersaredesignedforagroupofvirtuallycoupledHHTs
withtheobjectivesofreachingthesamespeedwhileminimiz-
ingtrackingintervals,in-trainforcesandenergyconsumption.
The multi-objective receding horizon optimization problem is
transformedintoanonlinearprogrammingformtosolve.The
effectiveness of the proposed method is verified by numerical
simulations, which demonstrate its excellent performance in
speed tracking, interval maintenance and minimizing in-train
force, even in the presence of random disturbances. It will
be our future work to further consider the highly nonlinear
operationprocessofheavy-haultrains,andtoproposeavirtual
coupling strategy that is more suitable for the cooperative
operation of HHT group with long formations.
