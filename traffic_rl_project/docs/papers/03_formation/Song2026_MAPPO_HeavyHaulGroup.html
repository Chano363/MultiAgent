Cooperative Control for Heavy-Haul Train Groups Based on Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning

首发时间：2026-05-26

Zongying Song 1   

Zongying Song (1985-), male, senior engineer, research interests: railway signaling and train control systems

Shuo Li 2    Xingzhong Wang 1    Yingze Yang 3   

Yingze Yang (Corresponding author) (1981-), male, M.S. candidate, research interests: intelligent transportation and datadriven modeling.

  • 1、China Shenhua Energy Company Limited, Beijing 100011, China
  • 2、School of Electrical and Information Engineering, Changsha University of Science and Technology, Changsha 410114, China
  • 3、School of Computer Science and Engineering, Central South University, Changsha 410083, China

DOI:10.61951/SciencepaperOnline.202605.0114

Abstract：To explore novel methodologies for enhancing the efficiency of heavy-haul railway transport systems, this paper investigates the intelligent cooperative control problem of multiple independent heavy-haul trains forming an operational group. The core challenge lies in achieving speed synchronization, distance maintenance, and overall energy efficiency optimization of multiple trains at the macroscopic operation level under strict safety constraints. Addressing the high-dimensionality, nonlinearity, and distributed decision-making characteristics of this problem, this paper proposes a fully data-driven multi-agent reinforcement learning framework. Abandoning reliance on precise analytical models, each train is modeled as an agent that autonomously learns cooperative control strategies through continuous trial-and-error interaction with the environment. This paper first establishes a detailed longitudinal dynamics model of heavy-haul trains as a high-fidelity simulation environment for agent interaction. Furthermore, a training framework is designed centered on the Multi-Agent Proximal Policy Optimization algorithm, supplemented by potential-field-based safety guidance rewards and multi-objective optimization rewards. Simulation experiments demonstrate that compared with the standard Multi-Agent Proximal Policy Optimization baseline, the proposed data-driven framework can significantly improve the speed synchronization accuracy of the group and reduce overall traction energy consumption while ensuring zero safety violations.\par

keywords： heavy-haul train train group cooperative control data-driven control

基于多智能体强化学习的重载列车群组协同控制

宋宗莹 1   

Zongying Song (1985-), male, senior engineer, research interests: railway signaling and train control systems

李烁 2    王兴中 1    杨迎泽 3   

Yingze Yang (Corresponding author) (1981-), male, M.S. candidate, research interests: intelligent transportation and datadriven modeling.

  • 1、国家能源集团,中国神华能源股份有限公司,北京 100011
  • 2、长沙理工大学 电气与信息工程学院,长沙 410114
  • 3、中南大学 计算机学院,长沙 410083

摘要：为了探索提升重载铁路运输系统效率的新方法,本文研究了由多列独立重载列车组成运营编组的智能协同控制问题。其核心挑战在于：在严格的安全约束下,于宏观运行层面实现多列车的速度同步、间距保持以及整体能效优化。针对该问题的高维、非线性及分布式决策特征,本文提出了一种完全数据驱动的多智能体强化学习框架。该框架摒弃了对精确解析模型的依赖,将每列车建模为一个智能体,通过与环境的不断试错交互来自主学习协同控制策略。本文首先建立了详细的重载列车纵向动力学模型,将其作为智能体交互的高保真仿真环境。此外,设计了以多智能体近端策略优化（MAPPO）算法为核心的训练框架,并辅以基于势场的安全引导奖励和多目标优化奖励。仿真实验表明,与标准的多智能体近端策略优化基准相比,所提出的数据驱动框架在确保零安全违规的前提下,能够显著提高列车组的速度同步精度,并降低整体牵引能耗。

关键词： 重载列车 列车组协同控制 数据驱动控制
