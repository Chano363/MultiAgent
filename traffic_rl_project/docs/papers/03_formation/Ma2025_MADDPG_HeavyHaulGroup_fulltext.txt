B. Improved goal-oriented MADDPG
In most group train speed profile optimization frameworks,
rewards are primarily concentrated on terminal objectives,
leading to sparse reward signals. With few intermediate re-
wards, purely exploratory strategies struggle to identify op-
timized trajectories that meet operational metrics. To en-
hance the applicability of RL in multi-train speed profile
optimization, this study integrates goal-oriented guidance and
a cooperative collision avoidance mechanism to refine the
learning algorithm.
• Goal-oriented guidance
During interaction with the environment, when an agent
fails to achieve its original goal, it can replay experiences by
hypothesizing alternative goalsconverting ”failed” experiences
into valuable learning signals. This mechanism innovatively
expandsrepresentationalspaceoftraditionalRL:systemstates
now explicitly encode target task information alongside en-
vironmental observations. The resulting state-goal-action triad

• Collaborative collision avoidance mechanism
During a speed curve search, agents often violate speed
limits or maintain insufficient leader-follower spacing (below
the minimum safety margin). Solely relying on negative re-
wards fails to guarantee operational safety. To address this,
a dual-layer safety strategy is proposed: (1) After each state
update, dynamic calculation of maximum/minimum safety
protection curves defines the range of executable actions. (2)
Action masking bounds action selection, ensuring subsequent
maneuvers are safe and feasible. This hierarchical constraint
enables proactive risk mitigation while preserving algorithmic
flexibility.
C. Training process
Thelearningprocedureoftheproposedgoal-orientedMAD-
DPG framework incorporates temporally correlated noise into
thepolicyupdatemechanismtoenhanceexplorationdiversity.
The intensity of this noise is progressively attenuated during
training, enabling agents to asymptotically converge toward
optimal policies. Furthermore, to mitigate overfitting risks,
a regularization term with weight decay is applied to the
criticnetwork,therebystabilizingthelearningdynamicswhile
preserving generalization capabilities.

for coordinating train groups across various stages between
stations during emergencies. An MIQP model is constructed
for the collaborative optimization of heavy-haul train groups.
An improved MARL algorithm is designed to derive driving
