An Optimization Method of Heavy Haul Train Driving Strategy
Based on Dueling DQN
Qida Si 1, Lu Jiang 2, Qi Wang 2, Youbin Su 3,Zhiming Xiao 3,Yuxuan Han4 and Ming Chai* 1
Abstract—The operation of heavy haul trains on long and
steep downhill is very challenging, it is difficult for drivers to
accurately grasp the timing of the application of circulating
air brakes. To address this problem, this paper proposes an
optimization method for heavy haul train driving strategy based
on the Dueling DQN algorithm. On the premise of ensuring
safety, the optimization goal is to improve the efficiency of train
operation and reduce the total time of air braking. On the
premise of considering the time-delay characteristics of train
air braking force, a multi-particle model of a heavy haul train
is established, and then the optimization problem of the train
driving strategy is transformed into a Markov decision process
(MDP). According to the characteristics of heavy haul train
operation, the key elements of Dueling DQN are designed, and
the reward function is set based on the optimization objective.
The simulation experiment is carried out in the scene of a long
and steep downhill. At the initial speed of 50 km/h, the average
speed of the whole train can reach 65.95 km/h. Finally, the
optimization effect of the DQN algorithm is compared to verify
the effectiveness of the proposed algorithm.
I. INTRODUCTION
Heavy haul railway plays a vital role in China ’s trans-
portation with its strong carrying capacity and extremely high
transportation capacity. With the rapid growth of China ’s
freight demand, the heavy haul railway is also developing
in the direction of long marshalling and large weight. At
present, the driving control of heavy haul railway in China
mainly depends on the driver ’s manual control. However,
the complex line environment of heavy haul railway makes
it much more difficult for heavy haul trains to drive than
ordinary trains, and it is easy to cause safety accidents by
relying solely on manual experience. In addition, in the

As an important branch of machine learning, reinforcement

interacts with the environment continuously, and gradually
learns the optimal action strategy in continuous trial and error.
Reinforcement learning can effectively adapt to the optimiza-

years, it has been widely used in video games [1], automatic
driving [2], robots [3] and other fields. When studying the
optimization problem of heavy haul train driving strategy,
traditional optimization algorithms such as mathematical pro-

are difficult to express complex air braking constraints, and
the optimal driving strategies are mostly non-long down-
grades. In the problem of heavy haul train driving strategy,
the train action is discrete and the state is continuous. It is
suitable for reinforcement learning to optimize, and can save
the complex modeling process. Compared with the traditional
optimization algorithm, it is more flexible.

II. RELATEDWORK
Automatic Train Operation (ATO) is a research hotspot in
the field of railway transportation. Its core modules include
train dynamics model, target speed curve optimization and
speed tracking control. The essence of heavy haul train
driving strategy optimization is to obtain a speed curve that
meets the optimization goal. The following sections will
introduce the research status of heavy haul train speed curve
optimization and manipulation optimization using reinforce-
ment learning.
A. Speed curve optimization of heavy haul train
Rao and Feng [4] designed the optimal feasible strat-
egy based on the actual freight railway line analysis, and
discussed the optimality and uniqueness of the analytical
solution of train energy-saving operation. Bai and Xiao [5]
proposed a multi-objective optimization model considering
many practical constraints, which was solved by nonlinear
approximation and quadratic programming (QP) algorithm,
and the optimal trajectory was obtained. Li [6] proposed a
time-limited multi-objective speed curve optimization model
to achieve energy saving, punctuality and stability of train
operation. Su [7] solved the problem of train trajectory opti-
mization with STC by mixed integer quadratic programming
(MIQP), and carried out actual case simulation research
with real data. Dong [8] analyzed the driving difficulties of
heavy haul trains in conventional sections and ramp sections,
established a driving strategy constraint model, designed a
train operation strategy generation method based on fuzzy
predictive control, and locally optimized the train operation
strategy of complex line sections to obtain the target oper-
ation curve. The above research on the optimization of the
speed curve of heavy haul trains has not deeply explored or
simplified the time-delay characteristics of air braking force.
Especially in the scene of long and steep downhill, most of
them are simulated by empirical formulas.
B. Train operation optimization based on reinforcement
learning
Based on the Q-learning algorithm, Zhu [9] constructed
a train eco-driving strategy for energy-saving goals, and
introduced definitions related to travel time and energy con-
sumption distribution in the state representation. The effec-
tiveness and robustness of the proposed method are verified
by deterministic and random environments. Due to the size
limitation of the look-up table in the Q-learning algorithm,
in the optimization problem with large state space, only the
problem of ’ dimension explosion ’ can be solved by reducing
the accuracy. Liu [10] proposed an intelligent control method
for heavy haul trains running on long and steep downhill
sections based on deep Q network (DQN) algorithm, and
carried out experimental simulation with actual data to verify
the effectiveness and robustness of the proposed DQN-based
control method, but it also simplified the air brake. Shang
[11] proposed a DRL method with a reference system (DRL-
RS) for active handling of constraints, where the reference
system is responsible for checking and correcting the learning
progress of the agent to avoid going farther and farther on the
wrong road. In order to achieve precise positioning and speed
tracking control of the train position, Wang [12] proposed a
method based on integral reinforcement learning (IRL) and
parameter identification.

For RL, selecting the appropriate hyperparameters can
improve training efficiency and prevent instability during
training.This paper explores the influence of learning rateλ
and exploration attenuation rategon the training effect. From
Figure 3a , it can be seen that when training 100,000 rounds,
λis 0.001 and 0.00001, the cumulative reward is increasing.
After 70,000 rounds, the model reward ofλ0.001 increases
faster, but whenλis 0.1, the reward increases slowly and
gradually stabilizes at about -18, which indicates that the
model may fall into a local optimal situation. Figure 3b
shows different effects ofg. Whengis 0.9, the exploration
rate decays too fast, and the model relies too early on the
estimated value of the current Q value, resulting in the agent
failing to fully explore all possibilities of the state space and
falling into the local optimal dilemma. Although the model
withgof 0.99995 increased slowly in the early stage, it

of training. Therefore, we chooseλas 0.001,gas 0.99995.In

V. CONCLUSION
In this paper, a driving strategy optimization method
for heavy haul trains based on Dueling DQN algorithm
is proposed. Considering the time-delay characteristics of
air braking, a multi-particle model of heavy haul trains is
established. Based on the optimization goal of improving
train operation efficiency and reducing the total time of
air braking, the reward function is set. The simulation is
carried out on the long downhill, and the effectiveness of
the proposed algorithm is verified by comparison with the
DQN algorithm. In order to improve the efficiency of model
training, the following two measures are taken : the choice
of time granularity is large, and 13 trailers are regarded as
one section for processing, which leads to the reduction of
simulation accuracy. These two problems need to be solved
in the future work, and the actual train operation strategy
should be further considered.
