# Language Features — Results Section

---

## Tense and Voice by Context

| Context | Tense | Voice | Example |
|---------|-------|-------|---------|
| Move 1 — data source | Past simple | Passive | *"Data were obtained for 2,958 members."* |
| Move 1 — table/figure reference | Present simple | Active | *"Table 1 presents the associations."* |
| Move 2 — reporting findings | Past simple | **Active preferred** | *"Results showed that…"* / *"We found that…"* |
| Move 2 — locating in table | Present simple | Active | *"As shown in Table 2…"* |
| Move 2 — commenting (general pattern) | Present simple | Active | *"Overall, risk was greater for frequent breachers."* |
| Move 2 — commentary (specific observation) | Past simple | Active | *"The most notable finding was…"* |

---

## Reporting Verbs for Results

**For stating the finding (past simple):**
*showed, revealed, demonstrated, found, indicated, displayed, obtained*

**For table/figure reference (present simple):**
*shows, presents, displays, illustrates, summarizes, lists*

**For commentary (present or past):**
*suggests, indicates, appears, seems, was, were*

---

## Lexical Signals by Move

### Move 1 — Preparatory information
- *Information on X was obtained for N participants.*
- *The following results address research question 1.*
- *Table 1 presents the [descriptive statistics / associations / frequencies].*
- *For the purposes of this analysis, participants were divided into…*

### Move 2 — Locating results
- *Table N shows / presents / displays…*
- *As shown in Table N / Figure N…*
- *The results are summarized in Table N.*
- *Figure N illustrates the distribution of…*

### Move 2 — Reporting results
- *Results showed / revealed / indicated that…*
- *We found / observed / obtained that…*
- *[N]% of participants / subjects…*
- *The mean score was… (SD = …)*
- *The difference was statistically significant (p = …, d = …)*
- *Factors associated with X were: A, B, and C.*

### Move 2 — Commentary
- *Overall, the most notable finding was…*
- *In general, [pattern] was observed.*
- *Notably, this result was consistent across…*
- *The only exception was…*
- *These results show / indicate that [descriptive pattern].*

---

## Precision Requirements

Results must be maximally precise. All statistics must be fully reported:

| Statistic type | What to include |
|---------------|----------------|
| Percentages | N and % (e.g., "31% (n = 916)") |
| Means | M and SD (e.g., "M = 22.4, SD = 5.1") |
| Comparisons | Difference, test statistic, p-value, effect size (e.g., "t(48) = 2.3, p = .03, d = 0.62") |
| Correlations | r value and p (e.g., "r = .45, p < .001") |
| Risk ratios | RR and CI (e.g., "RR = 5.02, 95% CI: 2.96–8.51") |
| Qualitative | Frequency of themes / categories with examples |

---

## Hedging in Results

Results require less hedging than other sections — report what was found directly.

**Use hedges only for:**
- Descriptive commentary on patterns: *"results appear to show…"*, *"this seems to suggest…"*
- Unexpected or borderline findings: *"the difference, while not statistically significant, was…"*

**Do NOT hedge the core result statement:**
- ❌ *"Results may show that females engaged in more play."* (they either did or didn't)
- ✅ *"Results showed that females engaged in significantly more play."*

---

## Common Errors

- ❌ Vague: "Results were significant" → "Results were significant (p = .02, d = 0.54)"
- ❌ No table reference: just describing numbers without pointing to the table
- ❌ Interpretation: "This is because X causes Y" → belongs in Discussion
- ❌ Literature comparison: "Unlike Smith (2020)…" → belongs in Discussion
- ❌ Present tense for findings: "Results show that females play more" → "Results showed that…"
- ❌ Passive for reporting: "It was found that…" is acceptable but "We found that…" is preferred
