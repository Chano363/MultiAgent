# Language Features — Method Section

---

## Tense and Voice

The Method section is dominated by **past simple passive** — this is the most consistent tense/voice pattern of any section in a research paper.

| Context | Tense | Voice | Example |
|---------|-------|-------|---------|
| What was done | Past simple | **Passive** | *"Data were collected from…"* |
| Who did it (if relevant) | Past simple | Active | *"Three investigators observed…"* |
| Definitions of key terms | Present simple | Active or passive | *"Physical play is defined as…"* |
| Established procedures | Present simple | Passive | *"Inter-rater reliability is calculated as…"* |
| Ethical statements | Past simple | Passive | *"Consent was obtained from…"* |

**Why passive dominates:** The Method section focuses on the procedure itself, not on the researchers. Passive removes the agent and foregrounds the action, which is the norm in scientific writing.

**Active voice is acceptable when:**
- The research team is the agent and the distinction matters
- The subject of the active sentence is a non-human instrument or system

## Lexical Signals by Move

### Move 1 — Sample / source description
- *The sample consisted of… / comprised… / included…*
- *Participants were recruited from… / selected via…*
- *A total of N [subjects/articles/texts] were included.*
- *Inclusion criteria were… / Participants were required to…*
- *The study was approved by… / Informed consent was obtained.*

### Move 2 — Procedure
- *The experiment was conducted… / carried out… / performed…*
- *Participants were asked to… / required to… / instructed to…*
- *[Instrument] was used to measure… / assess… / record…*
- *The procedure consisted of… / involved… / comprised…*
- *Each session lasted… / A total of N sessions were conducted.*

### Move 3 — Data description
- *Data were recorded using… / coded as… / stored in…*
- *Responses were transcribed… / coded… / categorized…*
- *A binary coding system was used, where 1 = … and 0 = …*
- *[Variable] was operationalized as…*
- *A total of N [responses/observations/words] were collected.*

### Move 4 — Analysis
- *Data were analyzed using [software/method].*
- *Statistical significance was set at p < .05.*
- *[Test name] was used to compare… / assess… / determine…*
- *Inter-rater reliability was established at [X]% agreement.*
- *Coding followed the framework proposed by [Author, Year].*
- *Descriptive statistics were calculated for each variable.*

---

## Precision Requirements

The Method section has the highest precision requirements of any section. Vague language must be replaced with specifics:

| Vague | Precise |
|-------|---------|
| "a large sample" | "a sample of 150 participants" |
| "participants were tested" | "participants completed a 20-item Likert scale measuring X" |
| "data were analyzed statistically" | "data were analyzed using a paired t-test (α = .05)" |
| "observations were recorded" | "each observer recorded presence/absence of X every 30 seconds" |
| "coding was reliable" | "inter-rater reliability was established at 90% agreement (κ = .87)" |

---

## Common Errors

- ❌ Present tense: "Participants complete a survey" → "Participants completed a survey"
- ❌ Active when passive is standard: "We collected data from…" → "Data were collected from…"
- ❌ Vague instrument description: "a questionnaire was used" → name it, describe its items, cite its source/validation
- ❌ Missing operational definitions of key variables
- ❌ Describing results in the Method section
- ❌ Missing ethical statement (for studies involving human participants)
