# APA 7th Edition — Detailed Rules

## General Rules
- Hanging indent (first line flush left, subsequent lines indented)
- Alphabetical order by first author's surname
- Author format: Last, F. M.
- Multiple authors: comma-separated, "&" before final author
- ≤ 20 authors: list all; > 20: list first 19, ellipsis (…), then last author

## Journal Article
```
Last, F. M., & Last, F. M. (Year). Title of article. *Journal Name*, *Volume*(Issue), Page–Page. https://doi.org/xxxxx
```
- Article title: sentence case only (first word + proper nouns)
- Journal name: title case, italicized
- Volume: italicized; issue: not italicized
- DOI required when available

**Example:**
Smith, J. A., & Lee, B. C. (2020). Effects of climate change on biodiversity. *Nature Reviews*, *15*(3), 120–135. https://doi.org/10.1038/xxx

## Book
```
Last, F. M. (Year). *Title of work: Subtitle if any*. Publisher. https://doi.org/xxxxx
```
- Book title: sentence case, italicized
- No place of publication (removed in APA 7)

**Example:**
Brown, L. (2019). *Understanding psychology: A comprehensive guide*. Academic Press.

## Edited Book Chapter
```
Last, F. M. (Year). Title of chapter. In F. M. Editor (Ed.), *Title of book* (pp. xx–xx). Publisher. https://doi.org/xxxxx
```

## Thesis / Dissertation
```
Last, F. M. (Year). *Title of dissertation* [Doctoral dissertation, University Name]. Database Name. URL
```

## Webpage
```
Last, F. M. (Year, Month Day). Title of page. *Site Name*. URL
```
- No author: move title to author position
- No date: use `(n.d.)`

**Example:**
World Health Organization. (2023, March 15). *Global health statistics 2023*. https://www.who.int/xxx

## Conference Paper
```
Last, F. M. (Year, Month Day–Day). *Title of paper* [Paper presentation]. Conference Name, Location. URL
```

## Report
```
Last, F. M. (Year). *Title of report* (Report No. xxx). Organization. https://doi.org/xxxxx
```

## Common Errors
- ❌ Book title not italicized
- ❌ DOI written as `doi:` instead of `https://doi.org/`
- ❌ Listing place of publication (removed in APA 7)
- ❌ > 20 authors listed in full (should truncate)
- ❌ Title case for article titles (should be sentence case)
