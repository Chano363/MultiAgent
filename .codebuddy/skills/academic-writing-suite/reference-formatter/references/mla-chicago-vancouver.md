# MLA 9th / Chicago 17th / Vancouver — Detailed Rules

---

## MLA 9th Edition

Primarily used in humanities (literature, language, arts).

### General Rules
- Section title: "Works Cited"
- Hanging indent
- Alphabetical order by first author's surname
- First author: Last, First; subsequent authors: First Last
- Article/chapter titles: in double quotes
- Book/journal/website titles: italicized, title case
- End every entry with a period

### Journal Article
```
Last, First, and First Last. "Title of Article." *Journal Name*, vol. #, no. #, Year, pp. #–#. DOI or URL.
```
- Page range: compress repeated digits — pp. 120–35 (not 120–135)
- Include DOI as full URL if available; otherwise include stable URL

**Example:**
Smith, John A., and Bob Lee. "Effects of Climate Change." *Nature Reviews*, vol. 15, no. 3, 2020, pp. 120–35. https://doi.org/10.1038/xxx.

### Book (Single Author or Multiple Authors)
```
Last, First. *Title of Book: Subtitle*. Publisher, Year.
```

**Example:**
Brown, Laura. *Understanding Psychology: A Comprehensive Guide*. Academic Press, 2019.

### Book Chapter in an Edited Volume
```
Last, First. "Title of Chapter." *Title of Book*, edited by First Last, Publisher, Year, pp. #–#.
```
- "edited by" in lowercase
- Page range refers to the chapter's extent in the book

**Example:**
Jones, Alice. "Memory and Identity." *Cognitive Perspectives in Literature*, edited by Mark Davis, Routledge, 2021, pp. 45–67.

### Conference Paper
```
Last, First. "Title of Paper." *Name of Conference*, Day–Day Month Year, Location. Publisher, Year, pp. #–#.
```
- If published in proceedings, treat like a book chapter within the proceedings volume
- If unpublished / presentation only, add "[Conference presentation]" before location

**Example (published proceedings):**
Chen, Wei. "Distributed Learning in Edge Networks." *Proceedings of IEEE INFOCOM 2022*, 2–5 May 2022, New York. IEEE, 2022, pp. 1234–42.

**Example (presentation only):**
Garcia, Maria. "Colonial Memory in Contemporary Fiction." [Conference presentation]. *Modern Language Association Annual Convention*, 6–9 Jan. 2023, San Francisco.

### Thesis / Dissertation
```
Last, First. "Title of Thesis." Degree type, University, Year.
```

**Example:**
Wang, Fang. "Urban Heat Island Effects in Chinese Megacities." PhD dissertation, Peking University, 2021.

### Webpage
```
Last, First. "Title of Page." *Website Name*, Day Month Year, URL. Accessed Day Month Year.
```
- If no author: start with title
- If no date: omit date field
- Access date required for frequently updated content

**Example:**
World Health Organization. "Global Health Statistics 2023." *WHO*, 15 Mar. 2023, www.who.int/xxx. Accessed 1 May 2023.

### Report
```
Last, First, or Organization Name. *Title of Report*. Publisher/Organization, Year. URL.
```

**Example:**
International Energy Agency. *World Energy Outlook 2023*. IEA, 2023. www.iea.org/xxx.

### Common Errors (MLA)
- ❌ Using "Ed." instead of "edited by" in book chapters
- ❌ Not compressing page ranges (120–135 should be 120–35)
- ❌ Missing access date for webpages
- ❌ Italicizing article titles (should be in quotes)

## Chicago 17th Edition

Primarily used in history, arts, and humanities. Two systems:
- **Notes-Bibliography (NB)**: footnotes/endnotes + bibliography — used in humanities
- **Author-Date (AD)**: in-text citations + reference list — used in social sciences

### NOTES-BIBLIOGRAPHY (NB) — Bibliography List

#### Journal Article
```
Last, First, and First Last. "Title of Article." *Journal Name* Volume, no. Issue (Year): Page–Page. https://doi.org/xxxxx.
```

**Example:**
Smith, John A., and Bob Lee. "Effects of Climate Change." *Nature Reviews* 15, no. 3 (2020): 120–135. https://doi.org/10.1038/xxx.

#### Book
```
Last, First. *Title of Book: Subtitle*. Place: Publisher, Year.
```
- Chicago retains place of publication (unlike APA 7)

**Example:**
Brown, Laura. *Understanding Psychology: A Comprehensive Guide*. New York: Academic Press, 2019.

#### Book Chapter in Edited Volume
```
Last, First. "Title of Chapter." In *Title of Book*, edited by First Last, Page–Page. Place: Publisher, Year.
```
- "In" capitalized; "edited by" lowercase
- Page range is the chapter's span

**Example:**
Jones, Alice. "Memory and Identity." In *Cognitive Perspectives in Literature*, edited by Mark Davis, 45–67. London: Routledge, 2021.

#### Conference Paper
```
Last, First. "Title of Paper." Paper presented at Conference Name, Location, Month Year.
```
- If published in proceedings, treat like an edited book chapter
- If available online, add URL at end

**Example (presentation):**
Chen, Wei. "Distributed Learning in Edge Networks." Paper presented at IEEE INFOCOM 2022, New York, May 2022.

**Example (published proceedings):**
Chen, Wei. "Distributed Learning in Edge Networks." In *Proceedings of IEEE INFOCOM 2022*, 1234–42. New York: IEEE, 2022.

#### Thesis / Dissertation
```
Last, First. "Title of Thesis." Degree type, University, Year.
```

**Example:**
Wang, Fang. "Urban Heat Island Effects in Chinese Megacities." PhD diss., Peking University, 2021.

#### Webpage
```
Last, First. "Title of Page." *Website Name*. Month Day, Year. URL.
```

**Example:**
World Health Organization. "Global Health Statistics 2023." *WHO*. March 15, 2023. www.who.int/xxx.

#### Report
```
Last, First, or Organization. *Title of Report*. Report No. #. Place: Publisher, Year. URL.
```

### AUTHOR-DATE (AD) — Reference List

#### Journal Article
```
Last, First, and First Last. Year. "Title of Article." *Journal Name* Volume (Issue): Page–Page. https://doi.org/xxxxx.
```

**Example:**
Smith, John A., and Bob Lee. 2020. "Effects of Climate Change." *Nature Reviews* 15 (3): 120–135. https://doi.org/10.1038/xxx.

#### Book
```
Last, First. Year. *Title of Book*. Place: Publisher.
```

#### Book Chapter
```
Last, First. Year. "Title of Chapter." In *Title of Book*, edited by First Last, Page–Page. Place: Publisher.
```

#### Conference Paper
```
Last, First. Year. "Title of Paper." Paper presented at Conference Name, Location, Month.
```

#### Thesis
```
Last, First. Year. "Title of Thesis." Degree type, University.
```

### Key Rules (Chicago)
- NB bibliography: first author inverted (Last, First); subsequent authors: First Last
- AD reference list: same inversion rule
- Both systems: alphabetical by first author surname
- Place of publication required (city; country/state if city is not widely known)
- Multiple authors ≤ 3: list all; > 3: list first author + "et al." (footnotes only — bibliography lists all)

### Common Errors (Chicago)
- ❌ Omitting place of publication
- ❌ Using Author-Date in-text style with Notes-Bibliography reference list (must not mix systems)
- ❌ "et al." in bibliography (should list all authors)
- ❌ Missing "In" before edited book title in chapter entries

## Vancouver Style

Used in medicine and biomedical sciences (ICMJE recommended).

### General Rules
- Numbered in order of citation: 1. 2. 3. (or superscript in text)
- Author format: Last FM — surname + initials, no punctuation between initials
- ≤ 6 authors: list all; > 6: list first 6 + "et al."
- No italics anywhere in Vancouver

### Journal Article
```
Last FM, Last FM. Title of article. Abbrev J Name. Year;Volume(Issue):Pages.
```
- Journal name: NLM standard abbreviation (e.g., N Engl J Med, JAMA, Lancet)
- No spaces in `Year;Vol(Issue):Pages`
- Page range may be compressed: 120-35

**Example:**
Smith JA, Lee BC, Wang XY. Effects of climate change on biodiversity. Nat Rev. 2020;15(3):120-35.

### Book
```
Last FM. Title of Book. Edition ed. Place: Publisher; Year. Total pages p.
```
- Omit edition if first
- Use "p." for total pages when citing whole book; use "p. xx-xx" for specific pages

**Example:**
Kasper DL, Fauci AS. Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine. 20th ed. New York: McGraw-Hill; 2018. 3900 p.

### Book Chapter
```
Last FM. Title of chapter. In: Editor FM, editor. Title of Book. Edition ed. Place: Publisher; Year. p. Pages.
```
- "In:" before editor(s)
- "editor" / "editors" after editor name(s)

**Example:**
Phillips SJ, Whisnant JP. Hypertension and stroke. In: Laragh JH, Brenner BM, editors. Hypertension: Pathophysiology, Diagnosis, and Management. 2nd ed. New York: Raven Press; 1995. p. 465-78.

### Conference Paper
```
Last FM. Title of paper. In: Editor FM, editor. Title of Conference Proceedings; Date; Location. Place: Publisher; Year. p. Pages.
```

**Example:**
Bengtsson S, Solheim BG. Enforcement of data protection, privacy and security in medical informatics. In: Lun KC, Degoulet P, Piemme TE, Rienhoff O, editors. Proceedings of the 7th World Congress on Medical Informatics; 1992 Sep 6-10; Geneva. Amsterdam: North-Holland; 1992. p. 1561-5.

### Thesis / Dissertation
```
Last FM. Title [dissertation]. Place: University; Year. Total pages p.
```

**Example:**
Wang F. Urban heat island effects in Chinese megacities [dissertation]. Beijing: Peking University; 2021. 180 p.

### Webpage / Electronic Resource
```
Last FM. Title [Internet]. Place: Publisher; Year [cited Year Month Day]. Available from: URL
```
- "[Internet]" tag required
- "[cited ...]" = access date

**Example:**
World Health Organization. Global health statistics 2023 [Internet]. Geneva: WHO; 2023 [cited 2023 May 1]. Available from: https://www.who.int/xxx

### Report
```
Organization Name. Title of Report. Place: Publisher; Year. Report No.: Number. Available from: URL
```

### Key Rules (Vancouver)
- No italics anywhere
- Semicolons between year and volume; colons before pages; no spaces: `2020;15(3):120-35`
- Citation-order numbering — never alphabetical
- Journal abbreviations follow NLM Index Medicus style
- "et al." after 6 authors (not after 3 like some other styles)

### Common Errors (Vancouver)
- ❌ Spaces in `2020;15(3):120-35`
- ❌ Fewer than 6 authors before using "et al." (threshold is >6, not >3)
- ❌ Full journal name instead of NLM abbreviation
- ❌ Italicizing journal names or book titles
- ❌ Missing "[Internet]" tag for webpages
- ❌ Missing "In:" before editor in book chapter entries
